    • Written by hand in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, libraries, website builders, or content management systems were used.
    • Progress, XP, and badges are saved only in the visitor’s own browser (local storage). There are no accounts, logins, or data collection.
    • Tested in current versions of Chrome, Edge, and Firefox and on a mobile phone.
